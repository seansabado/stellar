How to Pay

  1. Open the LaundromatAI x StellarPay app and sign in.
  2. Open your laundry order and tap Pay Now.
  3. When prompted, scan the merchant QR above to verify the shop.
  4. The app generates your personal payment QR — scan it with your Stellar wallet.
  5. App confirms payment on-chain and shows your receipt instantly.
⚠️ Include the exact payment memo shown in the app when sending from your wallet — required for automatic on-chain confirmation.
