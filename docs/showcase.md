# StellarPay One-Page Showcase

## Product Snapshot

LaundromatAI x StellarPay is a customer payment experience for laundromat operations.
It connects tenant-scoped order visibility, secure sign-in, Stellar payment confirmation, and receipt history in one flow.

## Enterprise Value Proposition

- Reduces payment friction for customers with fast scan-and-pay checkout.
- Improves trust with clear status transitions and receipt visibility.
- Preserves tenant boundaries for operational safety and auditability.
- Aligns with LaundromatAI branding and customer journey standards.

## What Judges Will See

- Customer sign-in and tenant-scoped orders.
- Checkout amount in Philippine pesos (PHP).
- Stellar payment status progression from pending to confirmed.
- Receipt persistence visible in payment history.

## 60-Second Demo Route

1. Open Orders.
2. Select demo-order-001.
3. Start checkout and show PHP amount.
4. Confirm payment status update.
5. Open History and show saved receipt proof.

## Proof Points

- Tenant isolation in customer flow.
- Identity-backed customer session.
- Transaction reference and confirmation timeline.
- Receipt record available after payment completion.
